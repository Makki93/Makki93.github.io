Support & setup

Questions, problems or feedback? Email us or use feedback in your TestFlight beta.

bavariantechworks@mailbox.org

Include the app version, build number, iPad model, iPadOS version and steps to reproduce the issue. Remove private photos and details from attachments. Never send your PIN, recovery codes or Apple sign-in details.

Ready in three steps

  1. Set up

    Create your operator PIN and an event. Choose layouts, stickers and your design.

  2. Check

    Check the camera, free storage and print route if needed. Test your equipment before the celebration.

  3. Start

    Start the event. Guests take their own photos; your PIN protects administration.

Camera, Wi-Fi and printing

Allow camera access for capture and local network access for QR downloads. If Wi-Fi fails, check whether devices may communicate; guest networks may block this. For printing issues, first make a test print using the intended AirPrint route. System completion alone does not prove a print reached paper.

Subscription & use

The subscription renews automatically unless cancelled in your Apple subscriptions. Cancellation leaves access in place until the confirmed expiry date. Restore purchases and Manage subscription are in the operator area. No additional GaudiKistn account is needed.

After expiry, existing photos remain available to view, share, export and delete. New captures, result edits and print jobs require valid access. Operations already started finish safely.
