Ready in three steps

  1. Set up

    Create your operator PIN and an event. Choose layouts, stickers and your design.

  2. Check

    Check the camera, free storage and print route if needed. Test your equipment before the celebration.

  3. Start

    Start the event. Guests take their own photos; your PIN protects administration.

What you need

  • An iPad with iPadOS 27.0.1 or later and camera permission.
  • Once access is confirmed, taking photos does not require internet. Purchase and restoration may require an internet connection.
  • For QR download, the iPad and phone must share a reachable Wi-Fi network. The current JPEG link expires within two minutes and is revoked on closing or Wi-Fi loss.
  • AirPrint requires a compatible print route. Direct USB printing is not supported. Printers, paper and networking are not included in the subscription.

Planned annual subscription

Eligible new customers are planned to receive one free calendar month after explicit confirmation with Apple, then €19.99 per year in the German App Store. A calendar month lasts 28–31 days. In other countries, the local Store price shown before purchase applies.

The subscription renews automatically unless cancelled in your Apple subscriptions. Cancellation leaves access in place until the confirmed expiry date. Restore purchases and Manage subscription are in the operator area. No additional GaudiKistn account is needed.
