Subscription & use

Planned annual subscription

Eligible new customers are planned to receive one free calendar month after explicit confirmation with Apple, then €19.99 per year in the German App Store. A calendar month lasts 28–31 days. In other countries, the local Store price shown before purchase applies.

The subscription renews automatically unless cancelled in your Apple subscriptions. Cancellation leaves access in place until the confirmed expiry date. Restore purchases and Manage subscription are in the operator area. No additional GaudiKistn account is needed.

After expiry, existing photos remain available to view, share, export and delete. New captures, result edits and print jobs require valid access. Operations already started finish safely.

Apple EULA

Apple's Standard EULA applies to the app. The planned subscription unlocks photo booth functions on supported devices using your Apple Account; Family Sharing is not initially offered. This is independent software, not photo booth rental.

Apple Standard EULA

Check the camera, network, storage and print route before each event. Not every printer combination has been tested. The beta may contain errors; keep separate backups of important photos. Statutory rights remain unaffected.

The event organiser or photo booth operator determines the purpose, legal basis, sharing and retention of guest photos. Inform guests and obtain any necessary consent. The app does not replace that responsibility.

Public beta in preparation

The public beta is not open yet. A join link will appear here after approval. An initial 50 places are planned. TestFlight purchases are free sandbox tests with accelerated durations.
